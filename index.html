LET'S KNOW ABOUT US

Kedai Kopi Nusantara adalah tempat untuk menikmati kopi single origin Indonesia dalam suasana yang nyaman dan santai.
Kami menghadirkan pilihan biji kopi lokal dengan cita rasa khas dari berbagai daerah di Indonesia, cocok untuk menemani waktu bersantai maupun berkumpul bersama.

THE ESSENCE OF NUSANTARA

Rooted in Origin, Refined in Craft

ADVANTAGE 1

100% Nusantara Single Origin Beans

Directly curated from trusted farmers in Gayo, Tana Toraja, Kintamani Bali, and Bajawa Flores without external variety blends.

ADVANTAGE 2

Fresh Weekly Roasting

Artisanal light-to-medium roast profiles every week to maintain the clarity of natural volcanic terroir notes in each bean.

ADVANTAGE 3

Certified Baristas & Precise Manual Brew

Expertly brewed by professional baristas using precise extraction techniques to highlight each cup's unique flavor profile.

ADVANTAGE 4

Calm & Cozy Atmosphere

A warm, minimalist space designed for deep focus, casual chats, and enjoying your favorite cup in absolute peace.
